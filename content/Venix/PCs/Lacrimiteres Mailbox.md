>Over our adventures, the party will receive and send multiple instances of mail. Below are recorded the notes that pertain to the entire party / relevant NPCs


### Fall 1435 - From Carlet
>Dear friends,
>By the time you receive this letter, you should have arrived in Camerath. I hope Miriam wasn't too rude and was able to give you something to do. Things have been quiet here in the Capitol since your departure, though efforts are fully underway to prepare for Founding Day festivities. If you've never experienced the Queen of the Festival competition before, you really must make an effort to attend one day, or maybe even participate. The show is simply marvelous. It is the one thing our venerated Emperor is interested in, after all. A grand party.
>
>But I write to you not only to share pleasantries. A number of nobles and merchants in the West have confided in me as of late that they've been suffering greatly from bandit attacks. From their telling, it seems as if this group appeared out of nowhere and began coordinating attacks on valuable but under-guarded shipments. Normally, I would say a little danger is good for business- why hire protection if the roads are perfectly safe, after all, but I have also received word from a little birdie of mine that these men are also taking people. That is something I do not approve of, in or out of my territory. I ask that you keep an eye out for this group. You may send me or Adarian a letter with any information. If it's urgent, the guild can facilitate the message. The last report I received placed them around Crossroads and south of Balm Sure. Do not do anything rash, but seek glory all the same. I look forward to seeing my investment in you pay off.
>
>Best Wishes,
>Duchess Leylin Carlet

### Fall 1435 - Response to Carlet
>Dear Duchess Carlet,
>We arrived safely in Camerath and assisted Miriam with ridding her mine of an undead infestation. Many of the mines in Camerath are being re-opened despite a lack of resources to be found within, leading to growing unrest among some of the populace - namely the Mining Guild.
>
>We may have started to uncover some of these bandits in Crossroads where it looked like a group may have been trafficking people, but the building burned down before we could investigate further. Our next stop will be Balm Sure where we’ll be sure to look into any additional word on these bandits and their ilk. Will send word upon any findings of import.
>
>We likely won’t make it back to the Capitol in time for the Founding Day festivities, but will look forward to next year. I personally am interested in partaking in the Queen of the Festival competition.
>
>We will ensure your investment in us upholds your expectations.
>
>With Best Regards,
>Isolde of the Lacrimiteres Party

### Fall 1435 - To Carlet
>Dear Duchess Carlet,
>
>Thank you for sending Adarian to assist us in Balm Sure. With his assistance we were able to quickly and without incident resolve the banditry issue in town, remove a center of human trafficking, and reassign the Bramblevale title after the previous holders absconded from town.
>
>While looking into the bandits, we found they were part of a more established group called the Scarlet Palm who are experienced in human trafficking. They originated in the North as remnants of a dissolved Tannery specializing in red pigments. Their more senior members thus have permanently red-dyed hands. If you recall, this is the same red as Kiran was wearing in the Capitol sewers (however she doesn’t seem to be involved here). We will continue rooting out this organization where we can as they represent a threat to Venix’s wellbeing.
>
>In Balm Sure we also found more “blighted” animals in the Savannah, similar to the dogs we encountered in the Capitol sewers. These animals were directly connected to a black ‘tumor’ buried in the ground, and disintegrated once the tumor was destroyed. We believe whoever is creating and burying these growths is connected to the incident in Jackson back in Summer. In case similar blights show themselves, be aware that any adventurers tasked with solving the issue need to find and destroy the buried mass at the epicenter of the blight. This mass can discharge malevolent magical blasts and earth quakes, so caution is advised.
>
>Thank you for your continued support. We will endeavor to continue putting our best foot forward throughout our journeys.
>
>With Best Regards,
>Isolde of the Lacrimiteres Party

### Fall 1435 - To Hux
>Hello Lord Hux,
>
>I know we haven’t gotten off on the best terms due to our group’s current alliance with Carlet, but I wanted to let you know of a recent development we’ve found regarding the “blighted” animals within the Empire. We initially responded to a request from you dealing with the town of Jackson, where we encountered blighted wolves which weren’t quite undead but were clearly rotting and disintegrated upon death. I’m sure you also remember the blighted dogs we fought in the ruins under the Capitol.
>
>While in Balm Sure, we encountered a similar situation with blighted lions and hyenas in the savannah. Upon closer inspection, the “blight” stemmed from a buried ‘tumor’-like mass in the wilderness. This mass was clearly organic, radiated dark energy, and had the capacity to fire of malevolent magical discharges and small quakes upon our approach.
>
>The affected animals seem to be mainly predators, but that could be a coincidence. We confirmed the blighted creatures were not fully undead, but required neither water nor food to stay “alive”. In case similar events arise, we wanted to ensure you had additional detail to best prepare anyone tending to them. When we next come back to the Capitol, I would like to speak with you to discuss some form of partnership to improve the Empire.
>
>Personally, I would also like to talk with you about your history with my father.
>
>Thank you for all your efforts to improve Venix. I look forward to our next meeting.
>
>Best,
>Isolde of the Lacrimiteres Party
