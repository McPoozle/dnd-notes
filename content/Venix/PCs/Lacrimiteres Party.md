## Adventure Guild Sponsorship
>After the [[Crystal Tear]] debacle under the Capitol sewers, the party chose to (at least temporarily) side with [[Duchess Carlet]]. The terms are as follows:
- Each party member receives 4,000 GP
- The party gets a supermajority (min 5 votes) vote on any decision related to use of the Tear prior to action taken
- Duchess Carlet will sponsor us in the Adventuring Guild
	- The better we perform as a group, the better Carlet's reputation and court standing
- The party is to keep Carlet informed of our movements and findings and vice versa
- The party has the ability to have a personal meeting with Carlet every 30 days
