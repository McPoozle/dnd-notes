>These animals are afflicted by a blight that causes them to decompose and ooze black goo, but are not affected directly by Undead-repellant spells. They do not eat or sleep but will attack other animals and people alike


## Discovery Locations
#### [[Jackson]]
> Wolves with black substance clinging to their bodies; their abdomens had been eaten away and had blackened paper skin. Their bodies falling apart as the blackness ate away at their carcasses. Fire did not stop them
> Found an empty burrow with a lingering stench of death. Size ~2ft in diameter, 4 ft deep. Boot print found near the hole

#### [[Balm Sure]]
>All animals but mainly [[Adventure Guild Quest Board#Quest Safari Cleanup|lions]] affected by a sickness at [[Balm Sure#Tim's Safari|Tim's Safari]]. The afflicted lions were lethargic until provoked. A central mound sent out waves of quakes accompanied by pillars of malevolent energy directed at individual people. The mound itself emitted a visible evil aura that made us feel sick as we approached. Upon digging it up, found a [[Blighted Animals#Buried Organ|black organ / tumor]] easily destroyed with a bout of Magic Missile, also destroying any affected animals. 
>	Hyena brought in for study wasn't alive or undead (unaffected by positive energy spells) but didn't eat or drink and continually leapt at researchers

#### Buried Organ
>At multiple locations we've found an empty hole where something was hidden or currently buried organ that once destroyed lifted the blight on the affected animals as they turned to dust. Organ itself emits malevolent energy and seems to have some kind of directive to destroy threats. Smells strongly of death and decay. Upon destruction is exploded with fleshy pieces and sends out a final wave of energy before completely disappearing
