## Lore

#### Description
> Roads and areas full of Fog; accessible by wandering in or through mirror-like portals, usually based in water. Possibly other ways to access. Once entered, the field of view is obscured and moving in any direction off a given path will loop back around

#### Other   
> According to the [[The Foglands#Old Woman|Old Woman]], the Foglands are owned by a wizard named [[The Foglands#Evador|Evador]] who has a Kamadan for a pet and the Foglands serve as a test for those that would serve him

>[[Duchess Carlet]] is having [[Duchess Carlet#Adarian|Adarian]] begin research on the Foglands based on our accounts but has never heard of it
>	Adarian has heard that the Foglands are the domain of a powerful wizard; used in bedtime stories to children warning that if they wander off in heavy fog they'll get taken there

## Entrances Discovered

#### On The Road
>Between [[Camerath]] and [[Balm Sure]]. Happen into the Foglands while on the main road. On first foray into the Foglands, came across multiple branching paths labeled "Test or Trial"; [[Lacrimiteres Party|the Party]] went through a test with a wall oozing blood and needed to fill vessels in a specific order. Gorrister was attacked by a Blood Pudding and the Party helped get it out of him. Next the Party passed a Trial against 3 Green Dragon Wyrmlings, and passed two more tests involving a number puzzle and going across an invisible bridge while blinded. At end entered a tournament Arena where we killed a large Kamadan

#### Hidden Grove
>The pond in the [[Impassable Woods#Hidden Grove|Hidden Grove]] temporarily became a portal to the Foglands. The pond had a mirror-like sheen and upon entering it, came back up in a mirrored pond in the Foglands. Surrounding the Pond were [[Adventure Guild Quest Board#Quest Missing Persons|6 people]] who had visited the grove and took a swim. While in the Foglands, don't get hungry or thirsty. 6 other bodies of people who tried to escape and were killed by monsters in the water: Turtle-crabs and a young River Dragon. 
>	Gorrister, Isolde, Shawaaf, Ai, Sysko end up in Foglands. Peltar stays in Grove. We kill the creatures and the Foglands-side pond is full of blood. Going through takes everyone back to the Grove, but the dead bodies are stuck in the Foglands
>After everyone exits, pond loses it's sheen and returns to a normal pond

#### Outlander Well
>While in the Towering Woods, the [[Towering Woods#Outlander Well|Outlander Well]] was made into a portal to the Foglands. Archway inside leading to the [[Dead Lands]]; a semi-undead swamp. Likely how the gators were getting so far up North. We destroyed the connection by collapsing the archway. Upon getting out of the Well, it still kept reflective qualities
