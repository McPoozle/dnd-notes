>   Guild for adventurers to complete [[Adventure Guild Quest Board|quests]]. for the greater good of Venix; up until recently, only specific people could submit quests However, [[Duchess Carlet]] changed the process such that anyone with money could submit a quest. As you rank up, groups / individuals are required to be Sponsored (past Emerald) by a noble. Many Adventure Guild members end up in Mercenary Work, such as with the [[MacMillan Guild]]

### Amenities

> While on an active quest, Adventurers get free boarding at the Inn associated with the Guild, if available

> Taxes required by Adventuring Guild members starting at 100GP per person, going up as rank up. This is halved if the group / individual is sponsored. Due at EOY (Winter 1)

## Rank Exams

> To advance into the next rank, adventuring parties need to complete a requisite amount of quests at their current (or one level above if in a group) rank followed by successful completion of a designated Rank Up Exam held by the Guild
