>   Guild for adventurers to complete [[Adventure Guild Quest Board|quests]]. for the greater good of Venix; up until recently, only specific people could submit quests However, [[Duchess Carlet]] changed the process such that anyone with money could submit a quest. As you rank up, groups / individuals are required to be Sponsored (past Emerald) by a noble. Many Adventure Guild members end up in Mercenary Work, such as with the [[MacMillan Guild]]; common for guilds to "poach" adventurers after all-inclusive quest ordinance put in place

## Amenities

> While on an active quest, Adventurers get free boarding at the Inn associated with the Guild, if available
> 
> Main locations have Teleportation circles to each Capitol; only available with approval as it's very pricey. Teleportation can cause temporary vertigo / nausea 

> Taxes required by Adventuring Guild members starting at 100GP per person, going up as rank up. This is halved if the group / individual is sponsored. Due at EOY (Winter 1)

> Guild has a primary leader; it's partially funded by the state but also a private org; price to open outposts went up a few years ago

>Guild demerits - failed requests can warrant demerits; too many demerits force de-ranking and possibility for adventurers to re-establish eligibility
>
>Guild takes investigation requests very seriously; don’t allow speculation, name-calling or “vibes”; reports facts as is
>
>Guild explicitly forbids adventuring perks including health insurance; no maidens

## Sponsorship Perks
>Sponsored groups have the ability to rank up past Emerald. Additional perks:
>- Halved Annual Tax requirement
>- Official group name on badges
>- Exclusive line at Guild locations for quests
>- Wagon rentals & storage only 20GP annually at any guild location
>- Direct requests for quests related to sponsor

## Rank-Up Exams

> To advance into the next rank, adventuring parties need to complete a requisite amount of quests at their current (or one level above if in a group) rank followed by successful completion of a designated Rank Up Exam held by the Guild. Have to be held in a Capitol (either main or ducal). Exam specifications are different for individuals vs groups

#### Amethyst Exam
> Trial by combat against magical beasts (constructs) kept in the Guild; Ruby-ranked+ Adventurers stand in as proctors
> 	Amethyst Rank Privileges / Responsibilities:
> 	- 500 GP Taxes (halved if sponsored)
> 	- Can take on higher-ranked quests

#### Ruby Exam
>Tests ability to work under adverse situations and problem solve. Have 4 levers: Purple, Green, Pink, and Red. On failures the lever sends an electric shock and troll golems emerge from the walls to fight us. Have to hold down all levers in specific order
