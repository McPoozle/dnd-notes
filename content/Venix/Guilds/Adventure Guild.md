## Amenities

> While on an active quest, Adventurers get free boarding at the Inn associated with the Guild, if available

> Taxes required by Adventuring Guild members starting at 100GP per person, going up as rank up. This is halved if the group / individual is sponsored. Due at EOY (Winter 1)

> Guild has a primary leader; it's partially funded by the state but also a private org; price to open outposts went up a few years ago

## Sponsorship Perks
>Sponsored groups have the ability to rank up past Emerald. Additional perks:
>- Halved Annual Tax requirement
>- Official group name on badges
>- Exclusive line at Guild locations for quests
>- Wagon rentals & storage only 20GP annually at any guild location
>- Direct requests for quests related to sponsor

---

## Rank-Up Exams

> To advance into the next rank, adventuring parties need to complete a requisite amount of quests at their current (or one level above if in a group) rank followed by successful completion of a designated Rank Up Exam held by the Guild

#### Amethyst Exam
> Trial by combat against magical beasts (constructs) kept in the Guild; Ruby-ranked+ Adventurers stand in as proctors
> 	Amethyst Rank Privileges / Responsibilities:
> 	- 500 GP Taxes (halved if sponsored)
> 	- Can take on higher-ranked quests
