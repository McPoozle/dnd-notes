## Venix Residents
> Main residents of Venix are a variety of humanoids (humans, elves, tielflings, gnomes, halfings, dwarves)

> Additional residents are the [[Kin]] groups made up of a mix of groups (such as Orcs, Goblins, Beastkin). More "monstrous" races are seen as non-citizens not given the same rights as the humanoids; largely relegated to smaller pseudo-townships or slum areas of larger cities

## Venix-wide Institutions
> Venix has a few largescale institutions that span across the dukedoms

##### Banks
> Version that are Noble-run with promissory notes that can be handed out from specific noble houses to cash in
> Separate set of Banks are Guild-run that are more spread out; money can be deposited or withdrawn from any associated bank and are directly connected to the Guilds (Adventuring payments can be direct deposited). There are 5 branches of Guilds where funds can be accessed

##### Habenero
> Similar to the far-away Mango Wasps, this eatery is a chain restaurant found in some larger cities across the continent offering a standard set of food fare

## Festivals

#### Founding Day
> Held on Winter 1 and celebrated with the ***Queen of the Festival Competition***> 

- Competition that takes place the week of the founding day festival
- Competitors, who are *not necessarily* women, compete in contests of beauty, intelligence, physical ability, and artistic skill to crown the Queen of the Festival on the last day
- The winner is granted a dance with the Emperor at the grand ball and retains the title for the next year, during which time they have rank equivalent to a viscount
- Participants must be noble or sponsored by nobility, and its a great honor for both the winner and the sponsor
    - Theres a lot of competition amongst people looking to climb the social ladder to be nominated, and nobles who are always trying to find that diamond in the rough candidate who will win and earn them glory
- Usually there are 20-30 candidates per year, so a little under half the active noble houses in the empire participate
