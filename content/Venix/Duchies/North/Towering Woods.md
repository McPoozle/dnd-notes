## Conflicts/Quests

#### Snow Gators
>Attacked by Ghoulish and Shadow Gators and a Headlight Gator, supported by a zombified weregator, humanoid skeletons, and evil snow rabbits.

#### Undead Attacks
>Attacks in the area by undead have increased in both the Old Growth and Outlander Well areas, but orcs haven't had manpower to check up on them

##### Old Growth
>Entanglement of living and dead trees with a thick canopy. Malicious energy surrounds the area and reports of undead; 2 days from Camp and 5 days to the Outlander Well from there. 
>	Smell of rot in the evergreens. We ran into more Headlight Gators and snow rabbits + skeletons. [[Lacrimiteres Party#Isolde|Isolde]] killed by a weregator but doesn't see any ill effects. Isolde also hit by wild magic and now only eats raw meat
>	
>	Run into Large Gators further into the woods and a Giant Triceratops skeleton with black veins of energy and a [[Blighted Animals#Towering Woods Old Growth Old Growth|black pulsating heart]]. Dino has the Malevolent Beam attacks. 
>	Rabbits that had been attacking are not usually hostile. Find many rotting away nearby; their guts are rotten and filled with black spores, but don't dust

##### Outlander Well
>Vertical cave occasionally filled with water; magical energy in the area and reports of undead. 2 days from Camp.
>	On the way between the Old Growth and the Well, accosted by undead orc skeletons; air heavy with malevolent miasma
>	At the well, run into a large skeletal man with a [[Lacrimiteres Party#Aster|flaming skull head]] set on battling Aster after calling him out for being a coward; sends skeletons and ghostly gators at us while Aster runs away and he gives chase. After defeating the creatures, well has a thin layer of ice giving it a [[The Foglands#Outlander Well|reflective surface]] leading to the Foglands and another portal to what we believe to be the [[Dead Lands]].

## Locations / NPCs

#### Orc [[Kin#Orcs in The Camp|Kin Camp]]
>Leader **Thunarga** getting ready to retire. All speak Orcish and Common but unlikely to talk to outsiders. Majority seem neutral or happy to see [[Lacrimiteres Party#Aster|Aster]]. Live in a permanent tent setup; Initation for younger members of the group every ~4 years involving pilgrimage to the [[Dead Lands]] (they used to live closer; chased out by Iomedae ~50 years ago) to kill undead and prove themselves warriors and become paladins of **Ragathiel**. Those that abstain from Initiation become Stricken and disallowed from using weapons or voting. Stricken are allowed to join later Initiations to remove their rank. 
>	Paladins have a sacred duty to fight off the undead in the East; characterized by the Empire as religious fanatics

##### Khark
>Thunarga's son and candidate for new leader position. Anti-Adventurers and wants to start a war to keep them out of the Camp's business. Wants to continue sacred traditions; the Empire is emboldened to further cut them off and Camp has been ineffective in stopping this "Stand with me in taking back our peace of mind". Has connections to a group willing to assist

##### Knodag
>Candidate for new leader position. Aster BFF. Was once a Stricken but joined a later Initiation. Wants to put thought and care into decision-making to ensure no greater strife becomes a consequence of action. Weary of starting additional conflict as the orcs could be used by the outsiders

##### Urzoth
> Young orc woman; has a crush on Aster and about to start her Initiation; bonded a little with [[Lacrimiteres Party#Isolde|Isolde]] while working out together

##### Orc Bar
>Serviced by Orc Baarman. Home of Nut Juice
