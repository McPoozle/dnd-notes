## Lore

#### Description
> Sleepy town nestled in the ruins of a larger city; in a state of disrepair with neglected farmlands and pastures. Closer to the center of the city places look lived in. Tanning hides and fabrics is the main source of income. Some industry and smaller shops and churches, all in similar states

#### Other   
>Dustwind used to be a pretty big city; a few decades ago Balm Sure (Bramblevales) started buying up a lot of surrounding land and size of the Earldom shrank; been in state of decline for some time; dusty as it *was* a forested area and deforestation needed to build city; used to have another name before Dustwind
>
>Bandit attacks have been quite severe and frequent, with the [[Venix Capitol]] not responsive for requests for aid. [[Scarlet Palm|Bandits]] kill people that fight back, and steal people away

#### Rumormill
>The Loudmantle nobles have issues with the [[Adventure Guild]] which is why [[Fallkirk]] & [[Crossroads]] don't have an outpost. Murrick is surprised Dustwind has an outpost due to cost; most Crossroads quests come to Dustwind
