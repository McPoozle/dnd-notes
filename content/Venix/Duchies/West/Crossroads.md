## Conflicts/Quests
#### McGilded Courtcase
> [[Lacrimiteres Party#Ai | Ai]] experiences their first trial - a murder trial associated with adultery and fraud charges involving Baroness August McGilded accused of murder, victim Mason (owed money to the Baron). Meet [[Smaller-Scale NPCs#Von Worth | Prosecutor Von Worth]]. Additional involved characters: Furst the Hatter, Beppo the carriage driver, Bruce the Banker, Gina a young stowaway. Ai wins the case with Furst and Bruce declared guilty of murder, hired by the Baron

 >[[Lacrimiteres Party#Peltar| Peltar]] finds a warehouse that seems to be involved in slave trade connected to a cave entrance outside of town.
