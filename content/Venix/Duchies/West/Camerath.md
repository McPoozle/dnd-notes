## Conflicts/Quests

#### Mine Cleanout
>Quest given to us by the [[MacMillan Guild]] to dispose of creepy crawlies in the mine
>	**Encounters**: Decapus abberations, Stirges, Mimic, Fachen, Ghouls, Zombies, Skeletons, Large undead Cave Lizard
>	**Loot**: bag of holding, spell scrolls, helmet, GP, Potions
>	Mine clearly used to hold a group of people from remnants of furniture found; been overrun by monsters and undead. Isolde got ghoul fever. Found a small divot at the end of a larger cavern with an (actually dead) skeleton of "Maxwell" holding a holy symbol of [[Lacrimiteres Party#Shawaaf|Pharasma]] and a letter addressed to an Olivia dated 27 years ago
>		Bandits had invaded the mine and turned into ghouls; miners inside also turned. Maxwell left buried money for Olivia under "the tree where you proposed"
>	We delivered the letter; Olivia had moved on with her life and was distraught at the news. Let us keep the money but we gave it to her daughter
>[[MacMillan Guild#Miriam MacMillan|Miriam]] surprised to hear of the bandit hideout and undead; Tapestry found inside Miriam believes to have the [[Balm Sure#Bramblevales|Bramblevale]] crest

## Locations / NPCs

#### Mines
>17 mines scattered on the outskirts of Camerath that were previously full of ore / gems and have since gone mostly dry. Been re-opened but are not profitable. Auction in Winter 1434 for rights for the mines faced by Miner's Guild protests over safety and lack of minable resources. When mines closed 37 years ago, Owners kicked out the Miners, some of whom became bandits and Miner's Guild formed

#### Duran
>Up-and-coming merchant. Owns some of the mines that had been inhabited by Goblins; chased them out
