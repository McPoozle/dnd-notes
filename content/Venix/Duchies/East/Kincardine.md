## Lore

#### Description
> Large and bustling city; Carlet not resting to make sure relief goes out for the disasters. Within a tenday most damage is being taken care of and life returning to normal

#### Other   
> East hit by natural disasters, likely stemming in part from the runes activated from the [[Adventure Guild Quest Board#Quest Cave Investigation|MacMillan Cave Ruins]]
> 	From the Adventure Guild: “If it’s not on fire it’s under water and if it’s not under water the earth has opened to swallow it up. The East is in shambles”; Fires, monster attacks, small pits / sink holes. Lots of flooding closer to The Lake
> 
> Weapons shortages in other parts of the Empire but not as much here
> 
> Charity initiatives started by the Duchess to assist in paying taxes, finding work, getting food
> 	Help center opened ~8 years ago; walk in and show proof of Eastern residence and explain problems to be directed to programs for assistance. Adje has heard good things about it. Outposts in every major city

>East *used* to be a lot more religious but shifted recently. Before Carlet took power the temples didn’t used to be shoved into an alleyway

## Locations / NPCs

#### Adventure Guild
>Main location for the [[Adventure Guild]]. Interior is mirror image of [[Winterholm]] location. Completely blank request wall when we arrive / temporarily closed down

#### Armory
>large singular armory and central spot to connect outposts. Can craft pretty much anything including enchanted weapons
>Lot of the armories have been consolidated under a single company. Have centralized command here with large forge; still losing some material to bandits but less than other parts of Empire

#### Religious District
>Relegated to a small alleyway with all temples cramped and small; Duchess' initiatives largely pushing out the temple-assisted aid
>	Most clerics in the city contracted by the Duchess and separate from temples with competitive terms
>	Recent changes pushed all temples together through residential rezoning

##### Church of Pharasma
>Small and seen better days; not falling apart but very empty with old interior

#### Hawthorn General Store
>Huge mega-store owned by [[Isolde's Exes#Viviane|Viviane Hawthorn]] with branded items

#### Cultural District
>Includes Art Museums, Theatres, pubs / bars, Auction House, and public parks
>	Public Parks managed by the Duchess to maintain cleanliness

#### Carlet Manor
>Large and elaborate building. Both old and well-maintained. Less damaged by the recent disasters. Has a large foyer but not overly ornate with fancy but cozy rooms.
##### Adje
>Carlet's best [[Duchess Carlet#Adje|catgirl]] is acting footman at the Carlet manor & tourguide. Usually posted in the [[Venix Capitol]] instead of Kincardine. Gets sad when mentioning her mom. Only family is a brother named Lucas who lives in the Capitol; they haven't talked in a while
