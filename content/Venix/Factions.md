The Empire stands at a crossroads. A weak and ineffectual leader has allowed corruption to take root in the once virtuous government. Slavery, racketeering, smuggling, and unrest run rampant. The people beg for change, aware that at any moment their lives may be destroyed by the evil consuming the land. 3 factions vie for power in this tumultuous landscape: the vicious Nobles, the pragmatic Legalists, and the idealistic Rebellion. The victor will claim the soul of the Empire for generations to come - and it seems the day of reckoning is fast approaching.
