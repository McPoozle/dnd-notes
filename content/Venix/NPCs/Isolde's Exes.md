> Isolde has had multiple romances over her years prior to joining the [[Lacrimiteres Party]]; these have all ended and shown to be turning points in each ex's life, even if Isolde had assumed things ended on an even note


### Viviane
> Drow woman from Isolde's hometown of [[Tormenta]]; has gone on to be an owner of a largescale general goods store chain. She and Isolde were childhood friends and dated early on for a while in 1415; bought the Hawthorn barony title / land and is extremely wealthy

### Mimn
> Dwarf Isolde met in [[Rusvile]] in 1420 and dated for just over a year while Isolde and her father were working at the [[Venix Overview#South | Understr]] residence

### Elliot
> Human woman who dated Isolde for 2 seasons in 1425 while Isolde was in the South, with Elliot hired by her father as a guard. Isolde left in the middle of the night after a tryst in the Impassable Woods

### Enron
>Silver Fox (older) male human wizard in Evenseed. Dated Isolde for 6 seasons in 1428. He specializes in communing with the fire plane and using gems and technology to hone its energy
>Currently residing in Winterholm working on automated maid robots for the nobility

### Anderson
>Male catfolk [[Kin|beastkin]] Isolde dated briefly for a few weeks while in a small Northern town in 1430. Anderson had orders to report to Timeston and they attempted a long-distance relationship but it fell off over time 

### Strizorsel
>Female tiefling who was working as an apprentice to Isolde's father in 1431; Strizorsel was at face a much more capable wizard that Isolde was, and Striz left abruptly after Isolde discussed her jealousy while under a trance meant to help break the walls holding her back. Striz ended her relationship after only 3 years and returned to [[Opren]]
