>Duchess Leylin Carlet is the ruler of the [[Venix Overview#East | East]] and head of the [[Factions#Nobles|Noble]] Faction. She is mostly well regarded by the peasantry under her jurisdiction, but has a reputation for being ruthless to get her way Resident **Girlboss**. While running the Eastern Duchy, spends a majority of her time in the Capitol acting as the ruler of Venix behind the scenes

___
## Lore
  
#### Description 
>Carlet is a tall human woman with fair skin and short red hair. Given she is a high-ranking noble, she dresses in fine clothing. Given her occupation, she is constantly busy & is thought to be the richest person in the Capitol


#### Characterization 
>Grand Vizier to the [[Emperor Johannes]], Carlet claims she rewards based on merit, treats her people well (and seemingly only her people). The peasantry think highly of her from her public works programs
>
>Has had those who go against her killed; this includes clerks guilty of embezzlement/mail fraud, and nobles who get too big for their britches ([[Bramblevales]])
>
>According to Adarian she is "a very open-minded individual"

> Despite the largescale dislike of kingroups, Carlet's close circle of employees includes multiple beastkin whom she seems to treat fairly


#### Other 
>Rumor has it that she murdered her parents to gain their title and position as Duke of the East. Unclear if this is true; her parents were incompetent rulers and died suddenly 19 years ago. Overnight she started making changes to the East
>
>Rumors that towns she takes over (such as with [[Jackson]], get their taxes halved and private guards installed) and sets up aid programs. In [[Tormenta]] these programs widely take over what the numerous Temples were previously providing, sequestering the Gods-given aid to smaller areas of the towns/cities
>
>Rumors she puts people out of business or force to sign bad contracts (happen to be people already disliked)
>
>Has outsized influence over the Emperor on top of managing the largest duchy (in terms of land) which is usually the most powerful of the four

___
### Goals
>Carlet wants to expand her power for the 'betterment of the Empire' by any means necessary. Similar to the other Faction leaders, she is vying for control of the [[Crystal Tear]]
 
### Conflicts/Quests
>Has tasked the party with gaining more renown and making something of ourselves to prove her sponsorship was the right decision. Tasked the party with heading to Camerath to meet with [[Miriam]]
