### Western NPCs
##### Earl Lugwyn
> Earl Eloine Lugwyn is an iron-haired elven woman; presides over her Earldom (which includes [[Evenseed]]) with grace and doesn't seem to be as pretentious as other nobles and seems to be in the [[Factions#Legalists|Legalist]] faction. Close to [[Lord Hux]] and has passed legislation with him to open the [[Adventure Guild Quest Board]] to anyone with coin
> 	From drunken dinner: Not overtly fond of the Duchess but doesn't dislike her; Emperor doesn't seem to care about the Empire, unlike his mother who took a frequent hand in legislation and appearances

##### Von Worth
> Prosecutor and Ai's court rival; Extremely attractive middle-aged human man with gray hair. Perfect record outside his cases with Ai involves. Has conducted trials vs. Ai in [[Crossroads]] & [[Winterholm]]

##### Eliza
>Ex-leader of [[Camerath#PETA|Camerath]]'s branch of PETA. Not very bright; her heart is *maybe* in the right place but has no worldly understanding and very self-centered. Big on her Almond Milk creation

##### Alfonso
>Player in the [[Balm Sure#Narrow Road Theatre|Narrow Road Theatre]] and student of [[Lacrimiteres Party#Isolde|Isolde]] in the ways of how to act

#### Crane
>First met in [[Balm Sure]] at the Auction House. Specializes in Art. Brother Robin. Family lost their noble title (Marigolds) to a bad investment. Keeping tabs on the [[Scarlet Palm]] bandit group up North as they have a track record of weaseling into town management . Personally familiar with Adarian . Looking for the man that cost them their noble title (a charletan sold them fraudulent art)

#### Robin
>Crane's younger brother. Prefers a free life of adventure to the standardization of being settled down. Likes to see to see how the people live. Largely raised in an orphanage after fall of their house; was very young when it happened. Run into him while in [[Tarrin]] as he was there for a job investigating something. Wants to connect more with his brother 
>	Hobbies: Knife throwing, reading (Adventure books)
>	Works for the Shadow Core
