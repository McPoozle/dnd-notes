## Lore

#### Description

> Includes "monstrous" races such as Orcs, Kobolds, Goblinoids, etc as well as beastkin who share humanoid and animal characteristics. Largely live in ruins and unincorporated territories; used to be more conflict between humanoids and kin until a signed Treaty with the Empire ~120 years ago for a mutual peace
> 
> Some beastkin live in towns / cities with humanoids

#### Characterization

> Varied as all peoples are; any disputes are treated on an isolated basis due to the Treaties signed between the Emperor and several kin groups

#### Other

> Current territorial war in the North. Kin were on the continent/empire probably prior to other races coming in; history books say members of Kin not considered citizens of the Empire as they are suspicious liars, thieves, and not to be trusted, with beastkin said to originate when “nice normal” folk interbred with unsavory types
